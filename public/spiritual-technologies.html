Solstice Rituals

Harnessing the annual cycles of the sun to align with your personal goals and achievements

The solstices are two special times of year when the sun seems to stand still before the Earth moves into a new phase of its annual cycle. These thresholds are used to calibrate yearly objectives and to plant spiritual seeds connected to what we want to produce in the coming phases.
